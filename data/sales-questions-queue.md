## Queue

1. Tell me about a time you closed new business through cold outreach — a cold call, cold email, or cold walk-in that led to a signed customer. What did you say to get the meeting?

2. What's your best target-crushing quarter or year — walk me through the number, what got you there, and what surprised you along the way?

3. How do you keep your calendar packed with meaningful sales appointments week over week? What's your actual outbound rhythm?

4. Walk me through how you use a CRM day-to-day. What do you track, what do you not track, and what makes CRM discipline actually drive strategy vs just being data entry?

5. You've been in ops-heavy roles historically. Why sales now, and what makes you think the transition is a fit rather than a stretch?

6. Tell me about a specific dealer or B2B partner relationship you built from a cold start into a major account. How did you earn the initial meeting and what turned it into recurring business?

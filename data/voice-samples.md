# Voice register samples

_Generated during session 2026-07-19. Ten writing prompts across four registers — micro / short / medium / long — designed to capture how Daniel writes in registers his 130-pair interview-answer corpus doesn't cover._

**Purpose:** these are NOT for `qa_pairs`. They are source material for extending `data/personalized-response.md` with register-specific voice guidance (Slack tone, customer-email tone, decision-memo tone, etc.).

**Workflow:** Daniel writes each response verbatim under the prompt. When done, we analyze all samples together and roll voice patterns into `personalized-response.md`.

## Medium register

### Prompt 6 — Incident post-mortem opener
> Open a post-mortem doc for an incident where a RefriTrak sync job double-billed a handful of accounts overnight. Write just the opening paragraph — what happened, who's affected, current status.

> double-billed accounts post-mortem. summary: on July 19th, 2026, an incident occurred that caused a sync job to double-bill 8 customers who met certain filter criteria. the bug has been isolated and fixed, and the customers have been issued refunds and notified of the mistake via email. because no account met this unique criteria via testing, the bug slipped into production. we'll be adding these additional account statuses to our testing pool to prevent this in the future.

### Prompt 7 — Decision memo
